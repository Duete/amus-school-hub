# St Maria Goretti Alumni reference

Source: https://www.stmariagorettikatende.sc.ug/alumni.html

The reference page frames alumni as a home for reconnecting, networking, mentoring current learners and recent graduates, supporting scholarships, professional development, staying connected with old friends, giving back to school projects and infrastructure, attending reunion events, and accessing school facilities and library resources.

The Amus Alumni page adapts those ideas to Mbale Progressive Secondary School and retains the school’s own identity and motto. Executive alumni and notable alumni are represented as clearly marked placeholders because the user will provide the names later. No external alumni names were copied or invented.
